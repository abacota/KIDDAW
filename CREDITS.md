# Sound credits

KIDDAW's recorded sounds come from free sample libraries. Each was trimmed, set to an even
level, and converted to small AAC files (in `public/sounds`) by the sound processing script.

| Sounds | Source | License |
|---|---|---|
| Studio drum kit | [Virtuosity Drums](https://github.com/sfzinstruments/virtuosity_drums) by Versilian Studios | CC0 (public domain) |
| Rock drum kit | MuldjordKit. Drum samples provided by [DrumGizmo.org](https://drumgizmo.org), via [FreePats](https://github.com/freepats/muldjordkit) | [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) |
| Clap, snap, stomp, chest pat | [sfzinstruments body_percussion](https://github.com/sfzinstruments/body_percussion) | CC0 (public domain) |
| Pat (thigh slap) | Pipira_Piraco on Freesound, sounds [860386](https://freesound.org/people/Pipira_Piraco/sounds/860386/), [860387](https://freesound.org/people/Pipira_Piraco/sounds/860387/), [860388](https://freesound.org/people/Pipira_Piraco/sounds/860388/) | CC0 (public domain) |
| Grand piano | Salamander Grand Piano V3 by Alexander Holm, converted to MP3 by Tone.js, then to AAC | [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/) |
| Violin, cello, flute, clarinet, trumpet, French horn, trombone | [VSCO 2 Community Edition](https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE) by Versilian Studios | CC0 (public domain) |
| Recorder | [VCSL](https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL), the Versilian Community Sample Library | CC0 (public domain) |
| Guitar (nylon string) | [FreePats Spanish Classical Guitar](https://github.com/freepats/spanish-classical-guitar) | CC0 (public domain) |
| Steel guitar | Martin HD28 by Jeff Learman, from the [Discord SFZ GM Bank](https://github.com/sfzinstruments/Discord-SFZ-GM-Bank) | CC0 (public domain) |
| Bass guitar | [FreePats Electric Bass YR](https://github.com/freepats/electric-bass-YR) | CC0 (public domain) |
| Double bass | [D. Smolken double bass](https://github.com/sfzinstruments/dsmolken.double-bass) | CC0 (public domain) |

Orchestra and band notes were pitch-checked when processed: notes more than a tenth of a semitone
out of tune were retuned, and a note whose pitch didn't match its file name was left out.

The CC BY sources must keep their credit wherever KIDDAW lists credits. The Sound check page
(`/sound-check`) shows them too.
