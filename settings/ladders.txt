# KIDDAW ladders and levels
#
# You can change this file without touching any code.
#
# Each element of music has a ladder of steps, lowest first.
# Each step is one line:   level: what students can do
# The level is where that step first opens. A level also opens every step below it.
# Lines that start with # are notes for people. KIDDAW ignores them.

Levels: K-1, 2-3, 4-5, Studio

== Rhythm ==
K-1: Steady beat; ta and rest; fast and slow
K-1: ta-ti
2-3: ta-ah; meter in 2 and 4
2-3: ta-ka-ti-ki
4-5: ta-ti-ki and ta-ka-ti; meter in 3; largo, andante, allegro
4-5: Syncopation and dotted rhythms; accelerando and ritardando
Studio: Swing, 6/8, triplets, any rhythm

== Melody ==
K-1: sol-mi
K-1: sol-mi-la
2-3: do-re-mi, then do-re-mi-sol-la (pentatonic)
2-3: Low la, low sol, high do
4-5: fa and ti: the full major scale
4-5: Minor and other keys
Studio: Every note, any key or mode

== Harmony ==
2-3: A drone: do and sol held under a melody
2-3: An ostinato: a short pattern that repeats underneath
4-5: Two chords: I and V
4-5: Three chords: I, IV and V; a bass line
Studio: Any chords, including minor chords

== Texture ==
K-1: Thin and thick: add and remove layers
2-3: Melody and accompaniment
2-3: Layers coming in and dropping out: build-ups and breakdowns
4-5: Rounds: the same melody starting again later
4-5: A countermelody: two melodies at once
Studio: Balance: choose which layer is in front

== Timbre ==
K-1: Choose instruments by family; a drum kit or body percussion
K-1: Free play: play any instrument live, no song needed
2-3: Shape a sound in kid words, like bright or dark, short or long; effects like echo, big room, robot, underwater
4-5: Record your own sound and play it as an instrument
Studio: Design a sound from scratch

== Dynamics ==
K-1: Forte and piano
2-3: pp to ff
2-3: Crescendo and decrescendo
4-5: Accents; one layer louder or softer than the others
Studio: Draw the dynamics across a whole song

== Form ==
K-1: Same and different: A and B
2-3: ABA
4-5: Rondo (ABACA); intro and coda
4-5: Verse and chorus; theme and variations (A, A', A'')
Studio: Any form; copy and change sections freely
